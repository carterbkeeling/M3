m³ wordmark candidates

Phase 1 specimen (dev only), revised after the independent critique. Each mark is a set of constant-width stroke centerlines (src/brand/wordmark.ts) drawn with round caps and joins; every turn is an exact circular arc. Stroke width 10, m height 80, so at 16 px the stroke is exactly the UI's 2 px, stems land on whole pixels and every mark is a whole number of pixels wide. Heights are the m's height in CSS px. Each candidate also shows its loader (frames over the loader's timeline) and a side-by-side with a schematic of the Muni worm, drawn from its traits only (the real logo stays out of the repo). Colors: bone #F3EFE7, graphite #15161A, Pipe Cyan #12D6FF; never red.
